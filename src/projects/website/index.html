This website serves as a way for people to quickly find how to contact me, view my projects, and view a quick summary of who I am. Originally, I was going to make a guns.lol page, or a linktr.ee page. However, I discovered that cloudflare lets you host a domain under their "pages" system. Therefore I decided to try and learn HTML and CSS in order to put together my own site. This gave me control over how everything looked, while also teaching me important concepts about Web Development, such as: what you need to make a website, how you can structure and design your website, and what frameworks are available to help you.

If you're looking to make your own website, I would recommend reading the documentation for Cloudflare Pages here: Cloudflare Pages Documentation↗
