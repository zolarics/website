This project was made so I could have a central page that people can visit in order to see all of my projects, view a quick summary of who I am, and see a list of ways to contact me. It's fun to have your own website since you get complete control over it. Originally I was looking into using a linktr.ee site or a guns.lol page, but I also wanted the thrill of building something. I would recommend building your own website to anyone who wants a project that can be as easy or as hard as you want it to be. The best part is you get a pretty cool award of having your own site that you have full customization over. This reward also grows depending on however much effort you want to put into it.

Overall, I learned some neat things about web development by doing this, such as how you should structure a site, and how a website is effectively an online file system. CSS and HTML are pretty cool, though it can get extremely confusing if you let the structure of your website get out of hand. I'm happy with how it came out for now, and I plan to update this site as I get new ideas and as I get better with the languages.
